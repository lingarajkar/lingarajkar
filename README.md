## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **security-minded DevOps / SRE engineer** with **9.5+ years** of experience designing automated, reliable, and secure engineering platforms across **AWS, Azure, Kubernetes, and Linux**. I've worked with Infosys, Accenture, GlobalLogic, and Amadeus Software Labs, leading DevOps teams and owning production platforms end to end.

- 🚀 **Platform engineering:** enterprise CI/CD with Jenkins Shared Libraries (Groovy), GitLab Blueprints, and GitOps with ArgoCD
- ☸️ **Kubernetes at scale:** AKS, EKS, PKS and bare-metal clusters (MetalLB + BGP), Helm, HPA, canary and rolling releases
- 🏗️ **Infrastructure as Code:** Terraform, Ansible, Bicep, ARM, AWS CDK, and CloudFormation, including drift handling
- 🔐 **DevSecOps:** SAST/DAST, SonarQube, Trivy, Twistlock, and CrowdStrike wired into pipelines; least-privilege IAM and secret management
- 📈 **Observability & SRE:** Prometheus, Grafana, Splunk, New Relic, and PagerDuty to cut MTTD/MTTR
- 🤖 **GenAI engineering:** production RAG copilots, conversational SQL, and AIOps log intelligence on Azure OpenAI and Amazon Bedrock
